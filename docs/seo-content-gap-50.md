# Content Gap Analysis + 50 Highest-Opportunity Topics — italytaxiservice.com

> Audited against ALL published content (June 2026): 59 blogs, 12 service pages, 15 airport pages,
> ~25 city pages, 12 route pages, 6 border pages, 30 attraction-transfer pages, ~20 beach-transfer
> pages, 5 tours. Every topic below is verified new (no cannibalization with published content),
> commercial/transactional, and mapped to one supporting service page for booking conversion.
>
> Difficulty: L = low (long-tail, weak competition), M = medium, H = high.
> Sorted overall by: Booking Intent → SEO Opportunity → Internal-Link Value → Conversion Potential.

---

## Gap Analysis — where the white space is

**Fully covered (DO NOT recreate):** taxi prices/booking/scams/tipping/credit-cards/Uber, money, visa, packing, insurance, safety, generic Italy transport, city transport guides (Rome/Milan/Venice/Florence/Naples), Civitavecchia→Rome, Livorno shore excursions, Rome cruise arrival, Fiumicino & Malpensa arrival guides, Fiumicino hotels, Milan→Lake Como, Cinque Terre transport, Milan airport options, child seats.

**Biggest unserved gaps (where these 50 concentrate):**
1. **Train-station transfers** — ZERO blog coverage despite huge intent (Termini→Fiumicino, Centrale→Malpensa, station→cruise port).
2. **Airport→resort route transfers** — Sorrento, Positano, Taormina, Lake Garda, Costa Smeralda, Puglia, Cefalù: none blogged.
3. **Cruise ports beyond Civitavecchia/Livorno** — Naples, Salerno, La Spezia, Genoa/Savona, Messina, Venice, Bari, Palermo, Trieste.
4. **Luxury / business / wedding / group** — service pages exist but have NO supporting blog content funneling to them.
5. **Arrival problems / flight delay / meet-and-greet / late-night** — the highest-anxiety, highest-conversion queries are unblogged.

**Internal-linking principle:** each blog links to ≥1 supporting service page + relevant airport/city/route/cruise pages, and the target page should link back (two-way) for authority flow.
